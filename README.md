## Bio
I love to explore the intersection of art, identity, and technology, creating work that communicates stories and perspectives through visual media as I am pursuing my Bachelor’s degree in computer science (with a concentration in software engineering) as well as a minor in animation and another minor in data science at Arizona State University. I think there is a lot in common between the arts and computer science - the portfolio building, the dread of not finding a job, and the creative aspect- but we can get into that another day. Currently, I work as a graphic design intern for Normal Noise, an ASU Barrett Honors College student-run magazine, where I work with artists and writers to share their stories. Some of my graphic design work can also be seen as flyers for the ASU League of Legend’s team. While this creative work is not the traditional route of a "future software engineer," I think this gives me an upperhand by giving me an outlet for creative thoughts and ideas. This also means that I am pretty familiar with the software that artists work with on a daily basis. I am hoping to further my education and work towards sharing more stories through digital mediums, such as animation and video games. The current niche that I am most interested in studying are computer graphic and animation software.

## Artist’s Statement
Tiana Nguyen’s artistic practice explores the capabilities of visual and interactive storytelling of identities and perspectives. By capturing the world around her through digital illustrations and fictional worlds, she hopes to share what is nearly impossible to fully capture, experiences. As Nguyen traverses the realm that exists between the arts and technology, her work reflects not only the worlds that we encounter everyday but also the worlds that could have been or were created. She strives to create works that connect people physically and emotionally and invites them to be curious of other perspectives
